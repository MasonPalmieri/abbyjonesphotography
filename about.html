Meet Abby

Hi, I'm Abby — so glad you're here.

I'm a wedding, couples, and family photographer with a deep love for the in-between moments — the laughter mid-toast, the quiet hand on a back, the look exchanged before anyone notices.

I'm based in Buffalo, New York, but spend part of every year living in Germany — so whether your story is happening in Western New York, the Bavarian countryside, or somewhere in between, I'd love to be the one to tell it.

My work is bright, candid, and editorial without being stiff. I want you to feel like yourselves in front of my camera, not like you're performing. That's where the real photographs live.

When I'm not shooting, you'll usually find me exploring somewhere new with a coffee in one hand and my camera in the other — always chasing better light and quieter stories.
